How this works

A backend is the part of a website that receives information and does work behind the scenes. This portfolio does not run its own server: Netlify provides that backend on its free Forms service.

When you press Send message, the browser securely sends your name, email, and message to Netlify. Netlify checks the hidden spam field, saves genuine submissions in this site’s Forms dashboard, and then shows the thank-you page. I can open that dashboard to read and reply to your message.
